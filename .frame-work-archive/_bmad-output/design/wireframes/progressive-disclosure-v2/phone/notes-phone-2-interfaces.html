Progressive Disclosure

On mobile devices, Via-Gent maximizes the editor screen real estate with a bottom navigation bar for quick access to other features.

This approach ensures:

  • Maximum writing space
  • Easy thumb reach for navigation
  • Context switching with one tap

The bottom tab pattern is familiar to mobile users and works great with progressive disclosure - show the current task, hide everything else.

Try it: Swipe left/right or tap the bottom tabs to navigate between notes, preview, and chat.
