**Title:** I built something that combines Notion + NotebookLM + Quizlet - and it runs in your browser

**Post:**

Ok I know title is clickbaity but hear me out.

I've been watching my non-dev friends struggle with too many apps:

- **Notion** for notes (but AI costs extra)
- **NotebookLM** for summarizing (but you can't edit properly)
- **Quizlet** for flashcards (but data stays on their servers)
- **Obsidian** for linking (but no AI built-in)

And I thought... what if there was ONE place where you could:

📝 **Take notes** - Block-based editor, voice recording, AI enhancement
🧠 **Build a knowledge base** - Import PDFs, URLs, let AI search and summarize
🎯 **Study smarter** - Auto-generated flashcards, spaced repetition quizzes
📱 **Use on your phone** - Actually works, not some "responsive" lip service

The kicker? All your data stays on YOUR device. No servers. No subscriptions. No "we changed our pricing" nightmares.

**What it's NOT:**
- A code editor for non-coders (though developers love it too)
- Another AI chatbot
- A cloud service

**What it IS:**
- A personal knowledge workspace
- AI that actually does work for you (summarize, organize, quiz you)
- Privacy-first (everything local)
- Free to start

It's called Via-Gent. Weird name, I know. But if you're someone who:
- Has 47 browser tabs open with "to read later" articles
- Forgets what you learned last week
- Wants AI help without another subscription

This might be for you.

Questions? I'm here. 👇
